## Google Drive Backup & Sync

Climbed-IT offers an optional feature allowing you to back up, sync, and restore your hill bagging logbook using your personal Google Drive account. 

If you choose to enable Google Drive Backup & Sync:
- **Authentication:** The app will request your permission to sign in with your Google account and access a dedicated app folder or backup file within your Google Drive.
- **Data Accessed & Stored:** The app only creates, reads, updates, or deletes the backup files that Climbed-IT itself creates in your Google Drive. It cannot view or access any other files, photos, or documents in your Google Drive.
- **How Your Data is Used:** Your Google account information (such as your email address or basic profile info used during sign-in) and backup data are used strictly on your device to authenticate you and transfer your hill log between your device and your personal Google Drive storage. ClimbedApps does not have access to your Google Drive, nor do we collect, view, or store your backup data on any servers owned by ClimbedApps.
- **Revoking Access & Deleting Data:** You can disconnect Google Drive sync at any time within the app settings, revoke Climbed-IT's access via your [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), or delete your backup files directly from your Google Drive.

**Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:**
Climbed-IT's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
